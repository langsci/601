First and foremost I am deeply grateful to Ruth Sima Rigamu who was my host and teacher during my 12 fieldwork stays between 2003 and 2014 in the village Hiovabon in the Autonomous Region of Bougainville, Papua New Guinea. She cared for my well-being in every respect and organized our local reseach team. I gratefully acknowledge the intensive cooperation of the authors, editors and proofreaders of this text collection, listed here in alphabetical order: Jubilee Kamai, Enoch Horai Magum, Helen Kobaa Magum, Shalom (Salome) Magum, Joyce Maion, Naphtali Maion, Paul Morekevan and Ondria Tavagaga.

Special thanks are due to the graphic artist Neville Vitahi whose illustrations are an invaluable contribution to the preservation of the Teop people’s knowledge of their traditional material culture.

My gratitude extends also to Ruth Saovana Spriggs who worked with me on the Teop language documentation project from 1994 until 2005, the research assistants Roslyn Purupuru, Jessica Reinig, Marciia Schwartz, and Yvonne Thiesen, and last but not least the funding organizations, The Australian Research Council (1994), the Volkwagen Foundation (2000--2007), and the German Research Foundation (2009--2011).

Finally, I would like to express my sincere thanks to Christian Döhler, the editor of the Open Text Collection, for his productive comments on the content and form of this volume and the transformation of the Word and ELAN files into this book.
