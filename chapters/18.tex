\begin{xltabular}{\textwidth}{lXX}
    (\ref{ex:text13-1})&\textit{O kovuaka eve to vatotomo nana bona a bua vaave vaa tea bua papa na inu.}&The top thatch sheet joins the two roofings on the two sides of the house together.\\
    (\ref{ex:text13-2})&\textit{A kiu nae he na paku vonaen:}&The work on it is done as follows:\\
    (\ref{ex:text13-3})&\textit{Eara huha rara a ta buaku a ta atovo, eara repaa vaakopo e toon no vatetekoio bono obai ge sareai.}&We sew two thatch sheets together, then we fix them as a cover on top of the upper ridgepole with (strings made of) the calamus cane or the \textit{sareai} vine.\\
    (\ref{ex:text13-4})&\textit{A bua ta atovo vaarii he toro bebeahu vaantee bona inu ge o vaatetekoio.}&These two thatch sheets must be as long as the house or the upper ridgepole.\\
    (\ref{ex:text13-5})&\textit{Eara repaa kisi taono e bono vaatetekoio, na kisi rara bono kara obai ge sareai.}&Then we tie them together on top of the upper ridgepole, using strings made of the calamus cane or the \textit{sareai} vine.\\
    (\ref{ex:text13-6})&\textit{Eara repaa mosi pete to pakoo.}&Then we cut an Albert Palm.\footnote{See \url{https://dictionaria.clld.org/units/teop-pakoo_1}}\\
    (\ref{ex:text13-7})&\textit{Eara repaa mosi kurusu vaantee a bebeahu na inu ge o vatetekoio.}&We cut it exactly according to the length of the house or the upper ridgepole.\\
    (\ref{ex:text13-8})&\textit{Erau eara repaa kopaa e, eara repaa kasi a komanae.}&Then we cut it lengthwise into halves and dig out its inside.\\
    (\ref{ex:text13-9})&\textit{Beara kasi vakavara e, eara repaa vaakopo taono mae bona bua ta atovo vai to paa vaobete vaevuru ara.}&When we have dug it out, we put the halves of the palm stem as a cover on top of the two thatch sheets that we have already laid there.\\
    (\ref{ex:text13-10})&\textit{Ei kokopo taono vai maa bona bua ta atovo,}&Now it covers the two thatch sheets,\\
    (\ref{ex:text13-11})&\textit{eara repaa kisi taono bana koa e bono vaatetekoio bono kara obai.}&and we tie it to the ridgepole with the calamus cane string.\\
    (\ref{ex:text13-12})&\textit{Evei a sii kiu vaa teo kovuaka.}&This is the little work of the top thatch sheet.\\
\end{xltabular}
