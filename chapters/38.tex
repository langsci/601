\begin{xltabular}{\textwidth}{lXX}
	(\ref{ex:text31-1})&\textit{Enaa kahi vahutate ni nom havee to suvasuva kokobus vaavaha vo raara.}&I am going to describe how we practice the fish poisoning with sea cucumbers.\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-2})&\textit{Beara tau nao tea suvasuva kokobusu, eara na dee rara o hoi, e ta rapisi a ta rutaa, ge o koopu rapisi.}&When we are about to go poisoning fish with sea cucumbers, we take a basket and a small knife or a bamboo knife with us.\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-3})&\textit{Beara suguna tea masi, eara repaa taneo tea avete kokobusu vo tea hoi.}&When we have arrived on the reef, we start collecting sea cucumbers into the baskets.\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-4})&\textit{Eara repaa kae vo tea kopua vai to pasi suva ni raara.}&Then we carry them to the pool where we want to use them for fish poisoning.\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-5})&\textit{O kokobusu to avete raara na dao riori bono pipimirari.}&The sea cucumbers that we collect are called Red Surf.\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-6})&\textit{Beara suguna tea kopua, eara repaa taneo tea kopaa a kokobusu.}&When we arrive at the pool, we start cutting the sea cucumbers lengthwise.\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-7})&\textit{Eara kopaa batara beara peo bata ni rae komana kopua bara tea vavarihi nae.}&We cut and scatter them over the pool and its side.\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-8})&\textit{Beo hoi kokobusu kavara, eara repaa nao habana koa tea avete.}&When the basket of sea cucumbers is finished, we go again to collect more.\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-9})&\textit{Eara repaa ma kopaa ae peo haabana koa e.}&Then we cut them and scatter them over the pool once again.\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-10})&\textit{Beara tara o iana beori taneo tea bubuutoo tavusu ae mate,}&When we see that the fish start struggling and die,\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-11})&\textit{eara repaa kona kou, a tahii paa peesi vaevuru.}&we know that the water has already become bitter.\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-12})&\textit{eara repaa antee tea kopaa kokobusu.}&and stop cutting sea cucumbers.\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-13})&\textit{Eara repaa taneo tea avete rio iana vai o matemate, vaobete teo hoi, eara repaa kae voosu eve.}&Then we start collecting the dead fish, put them into the basket and carry them home.\\
	(\ref{ex:text31-14})&\textit{Eve koa. Mataa.}&That's all. Thanks.\\
\end{xltabular}
