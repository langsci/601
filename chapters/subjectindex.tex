\chapter*{Subject Index}

This subject index is divided in six sections. \sectref{ind:palmtrees} and \sectref{ind:otherplants} list plants that are used for artefacts. \sectref{ind:nouse} lists plants that are mentioned by the authors, but have other or no uses. The Artefacts produced from plant materials are listed in six subsections of \sectref{ind:artefacts}. \sectref{ind:seashells} lists the seashells that are used for scraping rough surfaces and as parts of fishing nets, and \sectref{ind:tools} the white sand, the coconut husk and stones that are used as tools.

Apart from the English keywords, there are some keywords that are compounds formed by an English generic term, e.g. `palm', and a Teop specific term, when we did not know the English translation equivalent, e.g. `\textit{Kobuu} Palm'. The English keywords are followed by the Teop term in brackets, e.g. `Albert Palm' (\textit{pakoo}), which facilitates searches in the Multifunctional Teop-English Dictionary (\cite{Mosel:2019bt}).

If you are reading the PDF version of this book, the references below can be clicked and used like hyperlinks. For example, in ``Bengal Ficus (\textit{ruhu}) (\ref{chap:11}:\ref{ex:text7-80})'', the first number (11) links to the bilingual text in Chapter 11, while the second number (80) links to example line 80 in the glossed text, Chapter 40, Section 11.
