\begin{xltabular}{\textwidth}{lXX}
    (\ref{ex:text10-1})&\textit{\textbf{Taovu}}&\textbf{The giant pandanus}\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-2})&\textit{O taovu na neba ni raara beve a rutaa.}&We plant the giant pandanus, when it is small.\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-3})&\textit{Rerau ore paa pura hiava komana bata maa.}&And then it grows up by itself.\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-4})&\textit{Be pura hiava, a paka nae repaa bebeahu bata maa,}&When it grows up, its leaves become long,\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-5})&\textit{ore paa taneo mee iku gunaha.}&so that they also start hanging down.\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-6})&\textit{Beo taovu pura hiava vaantee, ore paa vakavakarapi.}&When the giant pandanus has grown up to a certain point, it branches out in all directions.\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-7})&\textit{Bean rake ta mosi hape to peho peana, ean repaa mosi e.}&If you want to cut off a branch, then you cut it.\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-8})&\textit{Rerau ean paa kosi a paka no taovu meno taovu tean.}&And so you cut off the leaves of the giant pandanus for your hood.\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-9})&\textit{Bean kosi vakavara a paka taovu, ean paa kaakaru e bono pesupaava, bean varoava bata nom eve.}&After you have cut off the pandanus leaves, you scrape off the thorns with a coconut husk, when you put them in the sun.\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-10})&\textit{Bea paka taovu tean meapa, ean paa aha e bono kakaamuru.}&When your giant pandanus leaves are flexible, you scrub them with white sand.\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-11})&\textit{Ean repaa varoava vahabana e.}&Then you put them into the sun again.\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-12})&\textit{Bea paka taovu tean kakaavoo, ean paa taatagi e ore paa matatopo vo tea kuna bono taovu.}&When your giant pandanus leaves have turned white, you prepare them, so that they are ready for sewing the hood.\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-13})&\textit{O taovu na kuna riori bono sigauva roge, na matatopo varaba komana riori.}&They sew the hood with a thread made of hibiscus fibres that they have prepared themselves in advance.\\
    (\ref{ex:text10-14})&\textit{O taovu na uvuuvu ni riori teo vuri to siko nana a huan.}&They wear the hood, when the rain is pouring down.\\
\end{xltabular}
