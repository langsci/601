\begin{xltabular}{\textwidth}{lXX}
	(\ref{ex:text25-1})&\textit{Enaa kahi vahutate ni nom a vagana vai to dao raara a ta Teapu bona tanaa.}&I am going to tell you a story about the fishing that we Teop people call \textit{tanaa}.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-2})&\textit{Bea ta Teapu tau nao tea tanaa, eori repaa dee bona maa kave teori tea masi vai to dao raara bono pasu masi.}&When the Teop people are about to do the \textit{tanaa}, they take their nets to the reef during the low tide that we call \textit{pasu} \textit{masi} `first low tide in the morning'.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-3})&\textit{A masi bona na tavutavusu nana teo pasu nubunubu be boobon vakisi nana.}&This low tide appears in the very early morning when it is still dark.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-4})&\textit{Tea matana takaha, a ta vaan repaa vaon bona maa kave teori tea maa sinivi,}&At the beginning of dawn, the people load their nets into the canoes,\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-5})&\textit{eori repaa hua vo tea hoa, ei beo puhana mei batana tea masi gunaha.}&and then they paddle to the passage before the tide has ebbed down.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-6})&\textit{Beori suguna, eori repaa nao busu vatanaa pina ni bona hoa.}&When they have arrived, they will go and throw their nets in a row, thus blocking the passage.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-7})&\textit{Ei vira vakavara ni rori bono kave, eori repaa sun teebona.}&Now when having dropped all the nets, they stand there.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-8})&\textit{Eori repaa vararana pina naori murina maa kave beori vahakaa pina batari.}&They will line up making a blockade behind the nets, while beating the water and blocking the passage.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-9})&\textit{Vahakaa rori bea iana nomaa nana, ei kou na gunaha maari tea hoa ore paa ma ona rori tea kave.}&They are beating the water while the fish are coming, and now they are coming down the passage and then get trapped in the nets.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-10})&\textit{Ei be ona, sa antee vaha haari tea nao, pasi topo koa nom komana kave.}&When they are trapped, they cannot go away, but will just be stuck in the nets.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-11})&\textit{Bea masi masi maa, ei be gunaha rakaha,}&When the sea is at low tide, when it has completely gone down,\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-12})&\textit{a tavaan repaa taneo tea pute bona iana komana kave }&the people start pulling the fish out of the nets \\
	(\ref{ex:text25-13})&\textit{to paa hapusu vaevuru.}&that are already full.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-14})&\textit{Beori pute vakavara bona iana vai to tei nana komana kave,}&When they have finished pulling out the fish that are in the nets,\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-15})&\textit{eori repaa kao hovo pete komana kopua, eori repaa tahi bona iana vai to paa pinou ei komana kopua bona.}&they will also wade through the pools in the lagoon and spear the fish that have been trapped in this lagoon.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-16})&\textit{Beori vagana vamatamataa rakaha bari, be kavara rakaha,}&When they have indeed fished every fish,\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-17})&\textit{eori repaa vaahae vahaa bona maa kave vo teo sinivi,}&they will load the nets into the canoes again,\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-18})&\textit{eori repaa voosu vahaa vo vaan mi bono sinivi iana.}&and return home with the canoes full of fish.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-19})&\textit{Meha papana rahi batari bona ibun iana to paa tahi vori tea maa makaree teori.}&Others thread the fish that they have speared with their fishing spears on strings.\\
	(\ref{ex:text25-20})&\textit{Eve o si vahutate no matapaku vaa tea tanaa ei teo kaku vagana vaa Teapu.}&This is the little story of the practice of \textit{tanaa}, this is a type of fishing in the Teop area.\\
